## Gig description

> Копіювати повністю. Перші два рядки — найважливіші, їх видно без розгортання.

```
Your team agrees on things in meetings. Then nothing happens.

Not because people are lazy — because nobody wrote it down, assigned it, or checked on it a week later. I fix that with an AI assistant that does it for you.

HOW IT WORKS

You paste a meeting transcript (or drop a recording). The assistant:

1. Pulls out every commitment made in that meeting — who promised what, by when
2. Creates a task for each one in your Notion workspace, with the exact quote from the meeting attached
3. Sends each person their own tasks in Telegram, with buttons: Done / Postpone / Not relevant
4. Updates Notion automatically when they tap a button
5. Gives you a weekly report: what was promised vs what was actually delivered

No new app for your team to learn. They already have Telegram. You already have Notion.

WHO THIS IS FOR

Agency owners, team leads, and founders who run 3+ meetings a week and are tired of being the human reminder system.

WHAT YOU GET

- A configured Notion database built for tracking commitments
- Your own Telegram bot, connected to your team
- One of your real meetings processed as a live test
- A 30-minute walkthrough call so you can run it yourself
- Support after delivery

WHY ME

I wrote this system myself. It is not a template I downloaded and rebranded, which means changes happen on my side rather than waiting on someone else's roadmap, and you are not locked into a tool you cannot get out of.

ABOUT YOUR DATA

I never ask for passwords. Notion access is a scoped integration token limited to one database. You create the Telegram bot yourself, on your own screen, and hold the token. When the job is delivered, I revoke my access and tell you I did. Happy to sign an NDA.

Message me before ordering and tell me two things: how many people are on your team, and where your tasks live now. I will tell you honestly which package you need — or if you do not need me at all.
```

## FAQ (Fiverr дозволяє додати — додайте всі)

**Q: Do I need to install anything?**
A: No. Everything runs inside tools you already use — Notion and Telegram. Your team just taps buttons in a chat they already have open.

**Q: What if we do not use Notion?**
A: The Premium package covers connecting a different tracker. Message me with what you use and I will tell you if it is supported before you order.

**Q: How accurate is it at pulling out tasks?**
A: It catches explicit commitments ("I will send the proposal Friday") reliably. Vague ones ("someone should look at the website") it flags and asks you who owns it, rather than guessing. You always see the list before anything is sent to your team.

**Q: Do you get access to our meeting recordings?**
A: Only what you send me for the one test meeting, and I delete it after delivery. After that the system runs on your side and I never see your meetings again.

**Q: What language do the meetings need to be in?**
A: English, Ukrainian, or Russian all work. Other languages are likely fine — send me a sample and I will confirm before you order.

**Q: What if my team ignores the Telegram messages?**
A: Then you will finally have proof of who ignores what, in the weekly report. That is usually the more valuable half of this system.
