## Шаблон заявки (proposal)

Тут зливаються 90% фрілансерів. Правила:

- **Перше речення — про клієнта, не про вас.** Якщо перше слово "I" — заявку не дочитають.
- **Не вітайтесь.** "Hi there, I hope you are doing well" = мінус два рядки уваги.
- **Доведіть, що прочитали вакансію** — процитуйте конкретну деталь з неї.
- **Довжина: 120–180 слів.** Довше не читають.
- **Одне питання в кінці.** Воно перетворює заявку на діалог.

```
You mentioned that [конкретна деталь з вакансії — процитувати майже дослівно]. That is
usually the part that breaks first, because [коротка причина, яка показує, що ви розумієте
проблему зсередини].

I build exactly this: meeting transcripts go in, and every commitment comes out as a task
with an owner, a deadline and the verbatim quote from the conversation — written straight
into Notion, then pushed to each person in Telegram where they confirm status with one tap.
The status syncs back automatically. You get a weekly report showing what was promised
against what was actually delivered.

I run this system on my own team, so I am not describing a plan — I am describing something
that is already working.

For [їхній розмір команди] people I would deliver this in [3-5] days, fixed price, with a

One question: where do your tasks live right now — Notion, a spreadsheet, or nowhere in
particular? The answer changes how I would set this up for you.
```

### Приклад заповненої заявки

Вакансія: *"We need someone to automate our meeting follow-ups. We use Notion and Slack.
Team of 12. Currently our PM manually types up action items after every call and half of
them get lost."*

```
Your PM typing up action items manually after every call is not the real problem — the
problem is that they are the only person who knows what was agreed, so when they are busy,
the whole follow-up chain stops. With 12 people, that bottleneck shows up every single week.

I build exactly this: meeting transcripts go in, and every commitment comes out as a task
with an owner, a deadline and the verbatim quote from the conversation — written straight
into Notion, then pushed to each person individually where they confirm status with one tap.
The status syncs back automatically. Your PM stops being the router and starts being a
reviewer.

I run this system on my own team, so I am not describing a plan — I am describing something
that is already working. Note that my current build pushes to Telegram; Slack is a
straightforward swap, and I would do that as part of the setup rather than as an extra.

For 12 people I would deliver this in 5 days, fixed price, with a training call at the end.

One question: does your PM want to keep final review before tasks go out to the team, or
should it send automatically? I would build it differently depending on the answer.
```

Зверніть увагу на два прийоми в прикладі:
1. **Чесно сказано, що зараз Telegram, а не Slack** — і одразу знято як заперечення.
   Приховати це = скандал на середині проєкту.
2. **Питання в кінці стосується їхнього процесу**, а не технічної дрібниці. Воно змушує
   клієнта уявити систему в себе.
